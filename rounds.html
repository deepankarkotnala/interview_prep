By interview round

The same question wants a different answer depending on who is asking. A recruiter wants thirty seconds and no jargon. A staff engineer in round two wants the mechanism and the trade-off you rejected. Filter to the round you have on Thursday.

RoundTypicalWhat it testsShape of a good answer
Screening 20–30 min, recruiter or junior engineer Whether the claims on your CV are real Thirty seconds. No jargon. One number. Stop talking.
Technical 1 45–60 min, senior engineer Fundamentals, and whether you built it yourself or watched someone build it Two minutes. Mechanism first, then one trade-off you actually made.
Technical 2 / design 60 min, staff engineer or architect Whether you can architect a system and defend the choices Requirements before technology names. Say the constraint you are optimising for.
Hiring manager 45 min Ownership, incidents, judgement, how you behave when it breaks A story with a decision in it, and a consequence you carried.
HR / fitment 30 min Stability, expectations, notice period Short and consistent. No negotiation detail here.
The mistake that costs most offers: giving the round-two answer in the screening call. The recruiter cannot evaluate depth, so a three-minute technical answer does not read as strong - it reads as someone who cannot judge their audience. Match the answer to the room.
