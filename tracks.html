By employer type

The job title is the same. The interview is not. What a company sells decides which half of your answer they care about — and preparing for the wrong half is the most expensive preparation mistake there is. Each track below gives the round structure, the ten questions most likely in that context, and one worked forty-minute scenario.

Five tracks Company names are examples of the type, not sources
Read this before the tracks. The companies named are examples of each type. Nothing here claims a specific company asked a specific question. What is honest, and useful, is the inference: an employer interviews about the system they actually run. A healthcare payer runs PHI through a locked Azure tenant, so that is what the panel presses on. That inference is sound. A leaked question list would not be.
