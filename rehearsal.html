Rehearsal room

Reading an answer and delivering it are different skills, and only the second one is tested. Draw a question, start the timer, and say the answer before you open it. If you stumble, you do not know it yet.

Rate yourself honestly. Nailed means you said the whole answer out loud, from memory, inside the target time, without a filler word every five seconds. Marking it because you understood it is how people arrive at an interview surprised.
