Reading an answer and delivering it are different skills, and only the second one is tested in an interview. Draw a question, start the timer, and say the answer out loud before you open the card. If you stumble, you do not know it yet.

How to score yourself honestly. Mark a question delivered only if you said the whole answer out loud, from memory, without reading, inside the target time, and without a filler word every five seconds. That is the bar in the room. Marking it because you understood it is how people arrive at an interview surprised.
