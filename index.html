Where these questions come from

No page here claims "this was asked at company X". Anything sold as a leaked company question bank is either invented or one person's recollection. These questions are built from:

  • Public job descriptions for GenAI, AI engineer and ML engineer roles at India centres.
  • The domain the employer works in. A healthcare payer asks about PHI because that is the system they run.
  • Published interview processes - round structure and duration that companies document themselves.
  • What the technology forces you to know. If you run retrieval in production you will be asked why it fails silently, because it does.

Every topic page carries its grounding, and cards with time-sensitive facts show when they were last checked. If you have real questions from your own interviews, add them to the data files with a date - that is how this turns from an inferred bank into a real one.
