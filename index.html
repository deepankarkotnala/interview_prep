An honest note on where these questions come from

No page here claims "this was asked at company X." Anything sold as a leaked company question bank is either invented or one person's recollection, and walking in prepared for the wrong interview is worse than walking in unprepared.

What these questions are actually built from:

  • Public job descriptions for GenAI, AI Engineer and ML Engineer roles at India centres - the panel is briefed from these.
  • The domain the employer works in. A healthcare payer asks about PHI and a locked Azure tenant because that is the system they run.
  • Published interview processes - round structure and duration that companies document themselves.
  • What the technology forces you to know. If you run retrieval in production you will be asked why it fails silently, because it does.

Every topic page carries its grounding label. If you have real questions from your own interviews, they belong in the data files with a date against them - that is how this turns from an inferred bank into a real one.
